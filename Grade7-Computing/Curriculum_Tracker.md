Source: official NaCCA CCP Computing JHS 1–3 (`curriculum/CCP-JHS/CCP_Computing_JHS.pdf`).
Codes `B7.*`. Grade 7 = JHS 1.

## How this tracker is ordered (deliberate reorder from NaCCA page order)

| Teach order | Sub-strand | NaCCA page order | Why I moved / kept it |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | **Components of Computers and Computer Systems** | 1 | You must know the machine before you can use or discuss it. |
| 2 | **Health and Safety in the Use of ICT Tools** | 3 → **moved up** | Labs and hands-on work start right after you know the parts. Safety must precede routine use. |
| 3 | **Technology in the Community** | 2 → **moved down** | Social impact and community tech make sense only once students can actually operate the tools. |

This order is **skills → safe practice → society**.

# Grade 7 Computing — Subject Rules

**The lesson pattern lives in [`RULES-BECE-PATTERN.md`](../../RULES-BECE-PATTERN.md).**  
Read it first. This file only adds subject notes.

## Subject notes (Computing / ICT, B7)

- Lesson body is **Theory Questions and Answers**: `Q. …? Ans. …` one sentence, or a bulleted list when the question asks for a list.
- Exercises are **BECE-shaped**: Paper 1 Objective (MCQ A–D, letter + reason) and Paper 2 Theory (marks + marking points).
- **Every** authentic bank question under the strand/sub-strand goes into the drills.
- Diagrams are **contextual** — computer components, workstation posture, "identify the parts" figures. Never decorate.
- Scenario openings belong in the exercise block only.
- Plain simple English, British notation, mandatory preambles.

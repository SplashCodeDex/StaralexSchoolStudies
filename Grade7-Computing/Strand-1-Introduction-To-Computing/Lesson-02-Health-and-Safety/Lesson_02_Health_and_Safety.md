## Preamble (pre-lesson)

Computers help us work, but they can also hurt the body if we ignore how we sit, look, and use the room.

This lesson answers questions about:
1. **Health measures** — how to protect your body.
2. **Safety measures** — how to avoid accidents.
3. **Applying health and safety in the lab.**
4. **Checking your workstation.**

**Words to know first:**
- **Health** — how well your body is.
- **Safety** — keeping yourself and others free from harm.
- **Posture** — the way you sit or stand.
- **Ergonomics** — arranging furniture so the body works comfortably.
- **Workstation** — your desk, chair, and computer as one set.
- **Surge protector** — a device that protects equipment from sudden electricity spikes.

---

## A. Health measures

**Q1.** What is health?  
**Ans.** Health is the condition of your body and mind.

**Q2.** What is posture?  
**Ans.** Posture is the way you hold your body when you sit or stand.

**Q3.** How should you sit when using a computer?  
**Ans.** You should sit with your back straight against the chair.

**Q4.** Where should your feet be when using a computer?  
**Ans.** Your feet should be flat on the floor.

**Q5.** How far should the computer screen be from your eyes?  
**Ans.** The screen should be about an arm's length from your eyes.

**Q6.** Where should the top of the screen be?  
**Ans.** The top of the screen should be at or just below eye level.

**Q7.** What is the 20-20-20 rule?  
**Ans.** The 20-20-20 rule says that every 20 minutes you should look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

**Q8.** Why should you follow the 20-20-20 rule?  
**Ans.** You should follow it to reduce eye strain.

**Q9.** How should your wrists be when typing?  
**Ans.** Your wrists should be straight when typing.

**Q10.** Why should you take short breaks when using a computer?  
**Ans.** You should take short breaks to rest your eyes, back, and mind.

**Q11.** What happens if the room light reflects on the screen?  
**Ans.** Glare appears on the screen and causes eye strain.

**Q12.** Why should headphone volume not be too loud?  
**Ans.** Loud headphone volume can damage your hearing.

**Q13.** Why should you wash your hands before using a shared computer?  
**Ans.** Washing hands reduces the spread of germs on shared equipment.

**Q14.** Mention three health measures when using a computer.  
**Ans.**
- Sit with good posture.
- Follow the 20-20-20 rule.
- Keep the screen at a safe distance.

---

## B. Safety measures

**Q15.** What is safety?  
**Ans.** Safety is keeping yourself and others free from harm.

**Q16.** Why should you not pull a plug by its cord?  
**Ans.** Pulling a plug by its cord can damage the wire and cause electric shock.

**Q17.** What is a surge protector?  
**Ans.** A surge protector is a device that protects equipment from sudden spikes of electricity.

**Q18.** Why should computer vents not be blocked?  
**Ans.** Blocked vents stop airflow and cause the computer to overheat.

**Q19.** Why should cables be kept tidy?  
**Ans.** Tidy cables prevent people from tripping and prevent damage to the wires.

**Q20.** Why should you not eat or drink at the workstation?  
**Ans.** Food and drink can spill and damage the equipment or cause electric shock.

**Q21.** What should you do if you see a damaged cable?  
**Ans.** You should report the damaged cable and not use it.

**Q22.** Why should you save your work often?  
**Ans.** Saving often prevents loss of work if the computer fails.

**Q23.** Should you share earbuds with a friend?  
**Ans.** No, you should not share earbuds because it spreads germs.

**Q24.** Mention four safety measures when using ICT tools.  
**Ans.**
- Do not eat or drink near the computer.
- Keep cables tidy.
- Do not pull plugs by the cord.
- Report damaged equipment.

---

## C. Health and safety in the lab

**Q25.** What should you do before starting a lab session?  
**Ans.** You should check the chair, screen, and cables before starting.

**Q26.** What should you do while working in the lab?  
**Ans.** You should sit with good posture and take short breaks.

**Q27.** What should you do when you finish working in the lab?  
**Ans.** You should save and close all files and shut down the computer properly.

**Q28.** Why should you push your chair in when leaving?  
**Ans.** Pushing the chair in keeps the lab tidy and prevents accidents.

**Q29.** Why should emergency exits stay clear?  
**Ans.** Clear emergency exits allow people to leave safely during an emergency.

**Q30.** Why should students be trained before using lab tools?  
**Ans.** Training helps students use the tools correctly and avoid accidents.

---

## D. Workstation check

**Q31.** What is a workstation?  
**Ans.** A workstation is your desk, chair, and computer used together as one set.

**Q32.** What is ergonomics?  
**Ans.** Ergonomics is arranging furniture and equipment so the body works comfortably and safely.

**Q33.** State four items on a workstation safety checklist.  
**Ans.**
- Screen at eye level.
- Chair supports the back.
- Feet flat on the floor.
- Cables tidy and not damaged.

**Q34.** Why does the room need good lighting?  
**Ans.** Good lighting reduces eye strain and glare on the screen.

**Q35.** How do you know if a workstation is safe?  
**Ans.** A workstation is safe when it passes every item on the safety checklist.

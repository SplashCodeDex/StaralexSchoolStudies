## 1. What this lesson is about

Four skills in one block, in the order you need them:

1. **Mental maths** — fast tricks with powers of 10 and number properties.
2. **Written four operations** — add, subtract, multiply, divide carefully.
3. **Decimals in operations** — same skills, with the decimal point to respect.
4. **Indices (powers)** — a short way to write $2 \times 2 \times 2$.

**Words to unpack first:**

| Word | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Power of 10** | 10, 100, 1 000, … (and 0.1, 0.01, …) |
| **Mental maths** | calculating in your head with strategies |
| **Digit / decimal point** | see Sub-strand 1 |
| **Repeated factors** | the same number multiplied several times |
| **Index (plural: indices)** | the small number saying how many times to multiply (the *exponent*) |
| **Base** | the number being multiplied |

## 2. Powers of 10 (B7.1.2.1.1)

Multiplying by 10, 100, 1 000 **shifts the decimal point to the right**.  
Dividing **shifts it to the left**.

| Operation | Example | What moves |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| × 10 | $3.4 \times 10 = 34$ | one right |
| × 100 | $3.4 \times 100 = 340$ | two right |
| ÷ 10 | $34 \div 10 = 3.4$ | one left |
| ÷ 1 000 | $3 400 \div 1\,000 = 3.4$ | three left |
| × 0.1 | $3.4 \times 0.1 = 0.34$ | one left (same as ÷ 10) |
| × 0.01 | $3.4 \times 0.01 = 0.034$ | two left (same as ÷ 100) |

## 3. Mental strategies (B7.1.2.1.2–1.3)

Use **rounding** and **compensation**:

**Example:** $48 \times 25$
- $25 = \frac{100}{4}$, so $48 \times 25 = 48 \times \frac{100}{4} = 12 \times 100 = 1\,200$.

**Example:** $198 + 47$
- $200 + 47 = 247$, then subtract 2 → **245**.

**Distributive property** (useful mental shortcut):

$$a(b + c) = ab + ac$$

$7 \times 102 = 7(100 + 2) = 700 + 14 = 714$.

**Word problem (B7.1.2.1.3):** A pen costs GH₵ 3. Buy 25.  
$25 \times 3 = 75$ → **GH₵ 75**.

## 4. Written four operations (B7.1.2.2.1–2.2)

### 4.1 Add / subtract up to four digits

```
  2 348
+ 1 756
------
  4 104
```

Line up the place values. Borrow/carry carefully.

### 4.2 Multiply and divide

**Multiplication:** $234 \times 12$

```
   234
×   12
-----
   468    (2 × 234)
  2340    (10 × 234)
-----
  2808
```

**Division:** $8\,064 \div 24 = 336$ (use long division; 24 × 336 = 8 064).

## 5. Decimals in operations (B7.1.2.2.2–2.3)

**Add / subtract:** line up the **decimal points**.

```
  124.3
    0.275
+  74.06
--------
  198.635
```

**Multiply decimals:** multiply as whole numbers, then place the point.
- Total decimal places in the factors = decimal places in the answer.
- $0.07 \times 0.02$: $7 \times 2 = 14$; 2 + 2 = 4 places → **0.0014**.

**Divide decimals:** make the divisor whole first.
- $4.86 \div 0.3 = 48.6 \div 3 = 16.2$.

**Story problems (B7.1.2.2.3):** A shirt costs GH₵ 47.50. Kofi buys 3 and pays with GH₵ 200.  
$3 \times 47.50 = 142.50$; change $= 200 - 142.50 = \mathbf{GH₵\,57.50}$.

## 6. Indices — powers of numbers (B7.1.2.3)

### 6.1 What $a^n$ means

$$a^n = \underbrace{a \times a \times \cdots \times a}_{n \text{ times}}$$

$2^3 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 8$.  
$a$ is the **base**, $n$ is the **index** (exponent).

### 6.2 Repeated factors (B7.1.2.3.1–3.2)

$24 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 = 2^3 \times 3$ (product of powers).

### 6.3 Zero as an index (B7.1.2.3.3)

Any non-zero number to the power 0 is **1**:
$$7^0 = 1, \quad 100^0 = 1$$

### 6.4 Value of a number in index form (B7.1.2.3.4)

$3^4 = 3 \times 3 \times 3 \times 3 = 81$.

### 6.5 Product of powers (B7.1.2.3.5)

$$a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$$

$2^3 \times 2^4 = 2^{7} = 128$.

Also useful:
$$a^m \div a^n = a^{m-n}, \qquad (a^m)^n = a^{mn}$$

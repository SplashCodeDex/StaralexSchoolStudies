# Grade 7 Mathematics
## Strand 1: Number
### Sub-strand 4: Ratios and Proportion
#### Lesson 1 — Ratio Language, Unit Rate, and Proportional Reasoning

**Codes:** B7.1.4.1.1 – B7.1.4.1.5

**Teaching order inside this block:**  
ratio language → unit rate → tables of equivalent ratios → proportional reasoning (missing values) → percent as a rate per 100.

---

## 1. What this lesson is about

**Ratio** compares two quantities: "3 pens to 5 pencils" is the ratio 3 : 5.

**Proportion** says two ratios are equal: $\frac{3}{5} = \frac{6}{10}$.

**Rate** compares different units: 20 km per hour, GH₵ 3 per pen.

**Words to unpack first:**

| Word | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ratio** | comparison of two quantities, same units |
| **Unit rate** | rate for **one** item (e.g. km per 1 hour) |
| **Equivalent ratio** | same relationship, scaled up/down |
| **Proportional** | one quantity grows at the same rate as the other |
| **Rate per 100** | another way to say percentage |

---

## 2. Ratio language (B7.1.4.1.1)

- Write ratios with `:` (e.g. **3 : 5**) or as fractions ($\frac{3}{5}$).
- **Simplify** by dividing both sides by the HCF: $18 : 24 = 3 : 4$.
- Order matters: 3 : 5 is **not** the same as 5 : 3.
- **Part to part** vs **part to whole**: if the ratio is 3 : 5, total parts = 8.  
  - Part to part: 3 : 5  
  - Part to whole: 3 : 8 and 5 : 8.

**Example:** In a class of 40, boys : girls = 3 : 5.  
Total parts = 8. Boys = $\frac{3}{8} \times 40 = 15$; girls = 25.

---

## 3. Unit rate (B7.1.4.1.2)

A **unit rate** is "per one".

**Example:** 250 km in 5 hours → $250 \div 5 = 50$ km per hour.

**Example:** 8 pens cost GH₵ 24 → GH₵ 3 per pen.

---

## 4. Tables of equivalent ratios (B7.1.4.1.3)

Scale a ratio up and down to build a table.

**Example:** 2 : 3 (apples : oranges)

| Apples | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Oranges | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |

Each row is an **equivalent ratio** ($\frac{2}{3} = \frac{4}{6} = \frac{6}{9} = \frac{8}{12}$).

---

## 5. Proportional reasoning — missing values (B7.1.4.1.4)

If two ratios are equal, use **cross-multiplication** or **scale factor**.

**Scale factor:** $2 : 3 \to 6 : 9$ (× 3).

**Cross-multiplication:**
$$\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} \implies a \times d = b \times c$$

**Example:** $\frac{x}{6} = \frac{3}{4}$
$4x = 18 \Rightarrow x = 4.5$.

---

## 6. Percent as a rate per 100 (B7.1.4.1.5)

30% of a quantity = 30 out of 100 = $0.3 \times$ the quantity.

**Example:** 30% of 250
$$\frac{30}{100} \times 250 = 75$$

**Share in a ratio:** share 720 in 2 : 3.  
Total parts = 5; one part = 144; shares = 288 and 432.

## 8. Eight exercise sets × 5 questions

### Exercise 1 — Ratio language
1. Simplify $18 : 24$.  
2. Ratio 2 : 3 as two fractions of the total.  
3. Boys : girls = 3 : 5, class of 40. Find boys.  
4. Write the ratio of 3 pens to 5 pencils.  
5. **[Review]** $\frac{3}{8} + \frac{1}{4}$.

### Exercise 2 — Unit rate
1. 250 km in 5 hours. Find km/h.  
2. 8 pens cost GH₵ 24. Cost of one pen.  
3. 120 km in 3 hours. Find km/h.  
4. 45 words typed in 5 minutes. Words per minute.  
5. **[Review]** $2^3 \times 2^4$.

### Exercise 3 — Tables of equivalent ratios
1. Complete: 2 : 3 → 4 : 6 → 6 : 9 → 8 : ?  
2. Complete: 5 : 2 → 10 : 4 → 15 : ?  
3. Is 6 : 9 equivalent to 2 : 3?  
4. Write a ratio equivalent to 4 : 5.  
5. **[Review]** Order $\frac{3}{8}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{5}{16}$.

### Exercise 4 — Proportional reasoning (missing values)
1. $\frac{x}{6} = \frac{3}{4}$. Find $x$.  
2. $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{x}{9}$. Find $x$.  
3. $y \propto x$; $y = 15$ when $x = 5$. Find $y$ when $x = 8$.  
4. 5 pens cost GH₵ 17.50. Cost of 8 pens.  
5. **[Review]** $124.3 + 0.275 + 74.06$ to 1 d.p.

### Exercise 5 — Sharing in a ratio
1. Share GH₵ 720 in 2 : 3.  
2. Share GH₵ 900 in 2 : 3 : 4.  
3. Two numbers in ratio 3 : 5, sum 96. Find them.  
4. Share 720 in 1 : 2.  
5. **[Review]** Value of 7 in 832 713.

### Exercise 6 — Percent as rate per 100
1. 30% of 250.  
2. 20% of 400.  
3. 35% of GH₵ 240.  
4. 15% discount on GH₵ 80. Find the sale price.  
5. **[Review]** $\frac{2}{5} \div \frac{14}{15}$.

### Exercise 7 — Real-world rates & scales
1. Recipe for 4 uses 300 g. For 10 people?  
2. Map 1 : 50 000. What does 4 cm represent in km?  
3. Which is larger: $\frac{2}{3}$ or $\frac{3}{5}$?  
4. 120 km at 40 km/h. Time taken.  
5. **[Review]** Round 12.473 to 2 d.p.

### Exercise 8 — Mixed / challenge
1. $\frac{2}{3} = \frac{x}{9}$. Find $x$.  
2. Share 720 in 1 : 2.  
3. $y = kx$; $y = 21$ when $x = 7$. Find $y$ when $x = 10$.  
4. **[Review]** $\frac{3}{8}$ of 480.  
5. **[Review]** $2^3 \times 2^4$.

## 10. Cheat sheet

| Skill | Rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| Simplify ratio | divide both by HCF |
| Unit rate | total ÷ number of items |
| Equivalent ratio | scale both sides the same |
| Missing value | cross-multiply or scale |
| Percent of quantity | $\frac{p}{100} \times \text{quantity}$ |
| Share in ratio | total ÷ parts, then multiply |

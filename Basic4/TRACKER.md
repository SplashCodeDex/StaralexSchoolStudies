### Month 1: Number Foundations, Operations & Fractions

#### Week 1: Place Value, Roman Numerals & Number Theory
- [ ] **Day 1: Multi-Digit Place Value & Number Modeling up to 100,000**
  - **Indicator:** B4.1.1.1.1
  - **Focus:** Preamble on digits vs numbers; place value columns (Units, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands, Ten Thousands, Hundred Thousands); base-10 block modeling; value vs place value.

- [ ] **Day 2: Reading & Writing Numbers in Figures and Words up to 100,000**
  - **Indicator:** B4.1.1.1.2

- [ ] **Day 3: Number Positioning, Ordering & Comparison up to 10,000**

- [ ] **Day 4: Rounding Whole Numbers & Estimation Strategies**
  - **Indicator:** B4.1.1.1.5

  - **Workspace:** `Strand_1_Number/Substrand_1_Counting_Representation_Cardinality/`

## Pedagogical Quality Standards (Rules.md Compliance)

1. **Plain Simple English:** Clear, direct explanations breaking down mathematical vocabulary before use (e.g. explaining "numerator", "cardinality", "highest common factor" with visual everyday analogies).
2. **British Mathematics Rules:**
   - Terminology: "Maths" (not "math"), "Highest Common Factor" / HCF (not "GCF"), "Lowest Common Multiple" / LCM (not "LCM"), "Brackets" (not "parentheses"), "Centimetres/Metres" (not "centimeters/meters"), "Cancelling down" fractions.
   - Date formats: DD/MM/YYYY.
3. **Preamble First:** No assumptions of prior mastery; each topic opens with an accessible grounding preamble.
4. **Visual Diagrams & Clean Formatting:**
   - Clear text/ASCII/vector diagram illustrations that do not break across viewports.
   - High-fidelity generated image illustrations where complex geometry or visual manipulatives are needed.
   - Professional PDF compilation with ReportLab (no deprecated APIs) preserving clean typography and vector tables.
5. **Rigorous Exercises with Spiral Review:**
   - 7 to 10 distinct exercise sets per sub-strand (5 questions per set = 35 to 50 questions total).
   - 10% to 20% cumulative spiral review of previously mastered strands.
   - Complete, step-by-step worked answers provided for every single question.

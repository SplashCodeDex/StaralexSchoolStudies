## 2. Preamble — Proportion Is Everywhere

If 1 kg of rice costs GH₵ 12, then 3 kg costs GH₵ 36.  
The **cost is proportional to the quantity** — double the rice, double the cost.

Three related ideas:

1. **Ratio** — comparing two quantities: 3 : 5.
2. **Proportion** — two ratios that are equal: $\frac{3}{5} = \frac{6}{10}$.
3. **Rate** — a comparison with **different units**: 20 gallons per minute, 80 km/h.

---

## 3. Ratio

### 3.1 Simplifying a ratio
Divide both parts by their HCF.  
$18 : 24 = 3 : 4$ (dividing by 6).

### 3.2 Sharing in a given ratio
Share GH₵ 720 in the ratio 2 : 3.

1. Total parts $= 2 + 3 = 5$.  
2. One part $= 720 \div 5 = 144$.  
3. Shares: $2 \times 144 = 288$; $3 \times 144 = 432$.  
Check: $288 + 432 = 720$ ✓

### 3.3 Ratio to fraction
$2 : 3$ means $\frac{2}{5}$ and $\frac{3}{5}$ of the total.

## 4. Direct Proportion

If $y$ is directly proportional to $x$ (written $y \propto x$), then $y = kx$ for some constant $k$.

**Find $k$ first, then use it.**

**Example:** $y \propto x$ and $y = 15$ when $x = 5$.  
$15 = k \times 5 \Rightarrow k = 3$. So $y = 3x$.  
When $x = 8$, $y = 24$.

**Money:** Total cost $T = p \times q$ (unit price × quantity).

---

## 5. Inverse Proportion

If $y$ is inversely proportional to $x$ ($y \propto \frac{1}{x}$), then $xy = k$ (constant).

**Classic: men and days.** More men → fewer days (same work).

$$\text{men}_1 \times \text{days}_1 = \text{men}_2 \times \text{days}_2$$

**Mock P1 Q6:** 9 men finish in 51 days. How long for 17 men?  
$9 \times 51 = 17 \times d \Rightarrow 459 = 17d \Rightarrow d = 27$ days. **Answer: C.**

## 6. Rates: Speed, Wages, Pumps

### 6.1 Speed
$$\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$$

**Mock P1 Q23:** 154 km in $1\frac{3}{4}$ hours $= 1.75$ h.  
Speed $= 154 \div 1.75 = 88$ km/h. **Answer: B.**

### 6.2 Unit-rate method (pumps, taps, printers)
**Mock P2 Q5(c):** 20 gallons/minute for half an hour (30 min).  
$20 \times 30 = 600$ gallons.

### 6.3 Wages and overtime
**Mock P2 Q3(a):** Mark earns GH₵ 8.25/hour for 8 hours, plus overtime at $1\frac{1}{2}$ times the regular rate for 4 hours.

- Regular pay: $8.25 \times 8 = 66.00$
- Overtime rate: $1.5 \times 8.25 = 12.375$
- Overtime pay: $12.375 \times 4 = 49.50$
- Total: $66.00 + 49.50 = \mathbf{GH₵\,115.50}$

### 6.4 Income over a period
**Mock P2 Q1(b):** Fred earns GH₵ 2500/month after tax. Eben earns **three times** as much.

- Eben: $3 \times 2500 = 7500$ per month.
- Combined monthly: $2500 + 7500 = 10\,000$.
- Five years $= 5 \times 12 = 60$ months.
- Total: $10\,000 \times 60 = \mathbf{GH₵\,600\,000}$.

## 7. Twenty Fully Worked Examples

### Example 1 — Simplify ratio
$18 : 24 = 3 : 4$.

### Example 2 — Share money
Share GH₵ 720 in ratio 2 : 3 → GH₵ 288 and GH₵ 432.

### Example 3 — Ratio as fractions
$2 : 3$ → $\frac{2}{5}$ and $\frac{3}{5}$ of the total.

### Example 4 — Direct proportion
$y \propto x$, $y = 15$ when $x = 5$. Find $y$ when $x = 8$.  
$k = 3$, so $y = 24$.

### Example 5 — Constant of proportionality (Mock P1 Q22 style)
Line through (2, 6) and (4, 12).  
$k = \frac{y}{x} = \frac{6}{2} = 3$ (also $\frac{12}{4} = 3$).  
**Answer: C — 3.**

### Example 6 — Inverse proportion (Mock P1 Q6)
9 men, 51 days → 17 men: $9 \times 51 / 17 = 27$ days. **C.**

### Example 7 — Speed (Mock P1 Q23)
$154 \div 1.75 = 88$ km/h. **B.**

### Example 8 — Pump rate (Mock P2 Q5c)
$20 \times 30 = 600$ gallons.

### Example 9 — Wages (Mock P2 Q3a)
$8.25 \times 8 + 1.5 \times 8.25 \times 4 = 66 + 49.50 = 115.50$.

### Example 10 — Income over years (Mock P2 Q1b)
$(2500 + 7500) \times 60 = 600\,000$ cedis.

### Example 11 — Unit price
5 pens cost GH₵ 17.50. Cost of 8 pens:  
$17.50 \div 5 = 3.50$ each; $8 \times 3.50 = 28.00$.

### Example 12 — Map scale preview
1 cm represents 4 m. 7.5 cm represents $30$ m. *(Full scale work in Lesson 10.)*

### Example 13 — Percent increase
A phone costs GH₵ 400. Price rises by 15%.  
New price $= 400 \times 1.15 = 460$.

### Example 14 — Percent decrease
A GH₵ 200 item is sold at 25% discount.  
Discount $= 50$; sale price $= 150$.

### Example 15 — Recipe scaling
A recipe for 4 people uses 300 g rice. For 10 people:  
$300 \times \frac{10}{4} = 750$ g.

### Example 16 — Combined work rate
Kofi paints a room in 6 hours, Ama in 3 hours. Together:  
$\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{2}$ of the room per hour → 2 hours.

### Example 17 — Fuel consumption
A car uses 8 litres per 100 km. For 350 km:  
$8 \times 3.5 = 28$ litres.

### Example 18 — Exchange / conversion
GH₵ 1 = 0.08 units of currency X. GH₵ 2500 = 200 X.

### Example 19 — Average speed round trip
Go 120 km at 40 km/h (3 h), return 120 km at 60 km/h (2 h).  
Average speed $= 240 \div 5 = 48$ km/h (total distance ÷ total time).

### Example 20 — Ratio with three terms
Share GH₵ 900 in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4.  
Parts $= 9$; one part $= 100$; shares $200, 300, 400$.

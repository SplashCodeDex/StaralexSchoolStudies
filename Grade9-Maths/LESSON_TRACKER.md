# Grade 9 Mathematics: Comprehensive Curriculum Map and Master Tracker

## Curriculum Architecture and Operational Standards

This master curriculum map and tracking framework is engineered specifically for the Grade 9 Mathematics syllabus, strictly adhering to the standards and pedagogical guidelines outlined in `Rules.md` and international British educational conventions.

### Core Pedagogical Directives (from Rules.md)
1. **Clarity and Simplicity**: Lessons must be delivered in plain, direct English with thorough, step-by-step conceptual breakdowns. Complex terms are unpacked thoroughly before abstract operations are introduced.
2. **Visual Learning and Diagrams**: Every concept requiring geometric, spatial, graphical, or structural intuition must feature clear visual aids. High-precision clean text-based coordinate and geometric diagrams will be used for standard representations; when spatial fidelity or visual complexity demands it, high-definition rendered visual illustrations will be produced via image generation.
3. **Structured 2-Month Timeline (60 Days)**: Paced across 8 academic weeks (approximately 60 study days), allocating 1 to 2 days per sub-strand or sub-strand part to prevent rushed learning.
4. **Rigorous Practice and Cumulative Spiral Review**: Following every completed sub-strand, a problem set of 7 to 10 distinct exercise sections (approximately 5 questions per section) is administered with full, worked solutions. Every new problem set incorporates a 10% to 20% quota of questions from previously covered strands and sub-strands to reinforce long-term memory.
5. **No Rushed Explanations**: Sub-strands with heavy conceptual depth are segmented into multi-day modules. A session only advances upon explicit review and confirmation.
6. **Workspace Organisation**: All study materials, notes, diagram assets, problem sheets, and interactive code playgrounds are modularised into designated strand and sub-strand directories.
7. **Strict Notation**: All terminology, spelling, and mathematical formatting follow British conventions (e.g., factorise, organise, cuboid, BODMAS, surds, indices, coordinates, standard form).

## Subject notes (CDT / CAD / Pre-Technical, B9)

- Lesson body is **Theory Questions and Answers**: `Q. …? Ans. …` one sentence, or a bulleted list when the question asks for a list.
- Exercises are **BECE-shaped**: Paper 1 Objective (MCQ A–D, letter + reason) and Paper 2 Theory (marks + marking points). BDT/Pre-Technical papers lean heavily on *sketch*, *figure*, *labelled parts* — those figures must appear.
- **Every** authentic bank question under the strand/sub-strand goes into the drills.
- Diagrams are **contextual and often compulsory**: tools, joints, orthographic/isometric drawings, design process. Never decorate.
- Plain simple English, British notation, mandatory preambles.

## Subject notes (Creative Arts and Design, B9)

- Lesson body is **Theory Questions and Answers**: `Q. …? Ans. …` one sentence, or a bulleted list when the question asks for a list.
- Exercises are **BECE-shaped**: Paper 1 Objective (MCQ A–D, letter + reason) and Paper 2 Theory (Section A Visual Arts, Section B Performing Arts: Music, Dance, Drama).
- **Every** authentic bank question under the strand/sub-strand goes into the drills.
- Diagrams are **contextual and often compulsory**: elements and principles of design, color wheels, shading techniques, musical notation, dance movements, stage configurations. Never decorate.
- Plain simple English, British notation, mandatory preambles.

## Subject notes (Computing / ICT, B8)

- Lesson body is **Theory Questions and Answers**: `Q. …? Ans. …` one sentence, or a bulleted list when the question asks for a list. Never bundle several questions into one.
- Exercises are **BECE-shaped**: Paper 1 Objective (MCQ A–D, letter + reason) and Paper 2 Theory (marks + marking points), including 10–20% cumulative spiral review from Grade 7 where helpful.
- **Every** authentic bank question under the strand/sub-strand goes into the drills.
- **B8 scope distinction:** Grade 8 owns the primary lessons for Fifth-Generation computers, Direct Data Entry (RFID/MICR), Specialist Output (Braille/3D printers), Flash/SSD storage, Drive Management, Assistive Tech, and **Desktop Publishing** (introduced in B8 under Strand 2 Sub-strand 3). Grade 9 handles these only via brief spiral review drills.
- Diagrams are **contextual** — add one only when the concept needs it (DDE hardware, publisher frame layouts, data communication models, Sense-Think-Act loops, ANN layers). Never decorate.
- Scenario openings belong in the exercise block only, and only when the real bank question uses one.
- Plain simple English, British notation. Preambles and "words to know first" are mandatory.

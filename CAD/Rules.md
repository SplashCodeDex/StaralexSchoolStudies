## Subject notes (Creative Arts and Design, B7–B9)

- Lesson body is **Theory Questions and Answers**: `Q. …? Ans. …` one sentence, or a bulleted list when the question asks for a list.
- Exercises are **BECE-shaped**: Paper 1 Objective (MCQ A–D, letter + reason) and Paper 2 Theory (Section A Visual Arts, Section B Music, Section C Dance & Drama). CAD papers heavily use *sketch* / *the figure below* / *labelled A, B, C* — the drills must carry those figures.
- **Every** authentic bank question under the strand/sub-strand goes into the drills.
- Diagrams are **contextual and often compulsory**:
  - **Grade 7:** design elements (dots/lines/shapes), balance/rhythm/repetition, shading on a form, clay/pulp hand-building, pattern techniques, treble staff & Curwen hand signs, performance space.
  - **Grade 8:** texture and colour, colour wheel, unity and variety, 2-D drawings/templates, printmaking & weaving patterns, musical forms, Ghanaian dance categories.
  - **Grade 9:** form and value, variety and proportion, 3-D drawings/models, casting/folding/assemblage, scale triads/arpeggios & I–VI–IV–V ostinato, one-act plot structure & stage blocking (DR, UL, DC; profiles & positions 1st–4th).
- Sketch questions ask for a drawing — the answer describes what the sketch must show and the figure demonstrates it.
- Plain simple English, British notation, mandatory preambles.

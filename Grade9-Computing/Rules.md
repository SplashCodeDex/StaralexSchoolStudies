# Grade 9 Computing — Subject Rules

**The lesson pattern lives in [`RULES-BECE-PATTERN.md`](../../RULES-BECE-PATTERN.md).**  
Read it first. It defines the Theory Q&A body, the Paper 1 / Paper 2 exercise shape, mark allocations, contextual diagrams, and the folder tree. This file only adds subject notes.

## Subject notes (Computing / ICT)

- Lesson body is **Theory Questions and Answers**: `Q. …? Ans. …` one sentence, or a bulleted list when the question asks for a list. Never bundle several questions into one.
- Exercises must be **BECE-shaped**: Paper 1 Objective (MCQ A–D, letter + reason) and Paper 2 Theory (marks + marking points).
- **Every** authentic bank question under the strand/sub-strand goes into the drills. Expand the quota rather than dropping an authentic question.
- Diagrams are **contextual** — add one only when the concept needs it (computer components, workstation posture, network topologies, "identify the parts" figures). Never decorate.
- Scenario openings (the *Mr. Agyekum…* style) belong in the exercise block only, and only when the real bank question uses one.
- Plain simple English, British notation. Preambles and "words to know first" are mandatory.
- 2-month pace, 1–2 sub-strands per day. Never rush. Split long sub-strands across days.

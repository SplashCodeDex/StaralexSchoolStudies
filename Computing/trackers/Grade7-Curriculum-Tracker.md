## Strand 1 Ordering Rationale (Deliberate Reorder from NaCCA Page Order)

| Teach order | Sub-strand | NaCCA page order | Why moved / kept |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 1 | **Components of Computers and Computer Systems** | 1 | Learners must know the machine before using or discussing it. |
| 2 | **Health and Safety in the Use of ICT Tools** | 3 → **moved up** | Labs and hands-on work start right after learning the parts; safety must precede routine use. |
| 3 | **Technology in the Community** | 2 → **moved down** | Social impact and community tech make sense once learners can operate the tools. |

This order is **skills → safe practice → society**.

# Grade 8 Creative Arts and Design — Lesson Tracker

**Source of truth:** [CAD-Lesson-Plan-B7-B9.md](../CAD-Lesson-Plan-B7-B9.md) → **VISUAL ARTS** section, Grade 8 listings (Design lessons are carried inside Visual Arts). The old NaCCA CAD PDF and its extracts were purged from the codebase; this tracker is built from the plan.

**Periods a week (Grade 8 focus):** Design — 1 period (50 min). Visual Arts — 2 periods (100 min). Music and Dance–Drama — alternate on the remaining period.

**Lesson numbering:** continuous 01–33 across the year: Visual Arts 01–18, Music 19–26, Dance Drama 27–33.

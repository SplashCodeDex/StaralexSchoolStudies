## Subject notes (CAD / Creative Arts and Design, B7)

- Lesson body is **Theory Questions and Answers**: `Q. …? Ans. …` one sentence, or a bulleted list when the question asks for a list.
- Exercises are **BECE-shaped**: Paper 1 Objective (MCQ A–D, letter + reason) and Paper 2 Theory (marks + marking points). CAD papers heavily use *sketch* / *the figure below* / *labelled A, B, C* — the drills must carry those figures.
- **Every** authentic bank question under the strand/sub-strand goes into the drills.
- Diagrams are **contextual** and often **compulsory**: design elements (dots/lines/shapes), colour wheel, shading on a form, joints, tools, isometric/orthographic sketches. Never decorate.
- Sketch questions ask for a drawing — the answer describes what the sketch must show and the figure demonstrates it.
- Plain simple English, British notation, mandatory preambles.

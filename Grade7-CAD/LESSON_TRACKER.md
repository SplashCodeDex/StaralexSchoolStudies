# Grade 7 Creative Arts and Design — Curriculum Tracker (NaCCA CCP / JHS 1)

Source: official NaCCA CCP Creative Arts and Design JHS 1–3 (`curriculum/CCP-JHS/CCP_Creative_Arts_and_Design_JHS.pdf`).
Codes `B7.*`. Grade 7 = JHS 1.

## How this tracker is ordered

| Teach order | Sub-strand | Why |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Design in Nature and the Manmade Environment** | Design thinking starts with **seeing** — elements and principles of design must be understood before they can be drawn. |
| 2 | **Drawing, Shading, Colouring and Modelling for Design** | Then **show** — apply the elements/principles through drawing, shading, colour, and 3D modelling. |

This is a **see → show** order. Students observe design first, then represent it.

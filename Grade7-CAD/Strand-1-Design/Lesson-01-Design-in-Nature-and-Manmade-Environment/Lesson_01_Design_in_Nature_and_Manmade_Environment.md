## Preamble (pre-lesson)

**Design** is how things are planned so they work well and look good.  
You can see design in **nature** (leaves, birds, rivers) and in the **manmade world** (buildings, roads, cloth).

This lesson answers questions about:
1. What design means and why it matters.
2. The **elements** of design: dots, lines, shapes.
3. The **principles** of design: balance, rhythm, repetition.

**Words to know first:**
- **Design** — planning how something looks and works.
- **Nature** — the natural world of plants, animals, rivers, and rocks.
- **Manmade** — things made by people.
- **Element** — a basic building block of design.
- **Principle** — a rule for arranging elements.
- **Biomimicry** — copying ideas from nature to solve human problems.
- **Pattern** — a repeated design.

## A. Design and why it matters

**Q1.** What is design?  
**Ans.** Design is planning how something looks and works.

**Q2.** What is nature?  
**Ans.** Nature is the natural world of plants, animals, rivers, and rocks.

**Q3.** What does "manmade" mean?  
**Ans.** Manmade means anything made by people rather than by nature.

**Q4.** Mention three reasons design is important.  
**Ans.**
- It solves problems.
- It communicates ideas.
- It expresses feelings and culture.

**Q5.** What is biomimicry?  
**Ans.** Biomimicry is copying ideas from nature to solve human problems.

**Q6.** Give one example of biomimicry.  
**Ans.** The beak of the kingfisher bird inspired the nose of the Shinkansen bullet train.

**Q7.** What manmade design did rivers inspire?  
**Ans.** Rivers inspired the design of winding roads.

**Q8.** What manmade design did anthills inspire?  
**Ans.** Anthills inspired house designs with natural air flow.

**Q9.** What natural design is similar to a fishing net?  
**Ans.** A spider web is a natural design similar to a fishing net.

**Q10.** How does design help learning?  
**Ans.** Design helps learning through clear posters, diagrams, and models.

## B. Elements of design

**Q11.** What are the elements of design?  
**Ans.** The elements of design are dots, lines, and shapes.

**Q12.** What is a dot?  
**Ans.** A dot is a single small point.

**Q13.** What is a line?  
**Ans.** A line is a dot that moves in a direction.

**Q14.** What is a shape?  
**Ans.** A shape is a closed outline you can see.

**Q15.** Give two examples of a dot in nature.  
**Ans.**
- Seeds
- Stars in the sky

**Q16.** Give two examples of a line in nature.  
**Ans.**
- Plant stems
- The edge of a leaf

**Q17.** Give two examples of a shape in nature.  
**Ans.**
- Petals
- Stones

**Q18.** What is a geometric shape?  
**Ans.** A geometric shape is an exact shape such as a circle, square, or triangle.

**Q19.** What is an organic shape?  
**Ans.** An organic shape is a free-flowing natural shape such as a cloud or a leaf.

**Q20.** Mention the three elements of design and give one example of each.  
**Ans.**
- Dot — a seed
- Line — a plant stem
- Shape — a petal

## C. Principles of design

**Q21.** What are three principles of design?  
**Ans.** Three principles of design are balance, rhythm, and repetition.

**Q22.** What is balance in design?  
**Ans.** Balance is when the parts of a design feel evenly arranged.

**Q23.** Give one example of balance in nature.  
**Ans.** The two sides of a face are an example of balance in nature.

**Q24.** What is rhythm in design?  
**Ans.** Rhythm is a sense of movement created by repeating elements.

**Q25.** Give one example of rhythm in manmade design.  
**Ans.** A row of windows on a building is an example of rhythm.

**Q26.** What is repetition in design?  
**Ans.** Repetition is using the same element again and again.

**Q27.** Give one example of repetition in cloth design.  
**Ans.** A repeated motif printed all over a cloth is an example of repetition.

**Q28.** How does repeating dots create rhythm?  
**Ans.** Repeating dots in a line makes the eye move along the line.

**Q29.** How do shapes create balance?  
**Ans.** Shapes create balance when they are arranged evenly on both sides.

**Q30.** What is a pattern?  
**Ans.** A pattern is a design that repeats in a regular way.

---

## D. Nature and manmade design together

**Q31.** How is a river similar to a winding road?  
**Ans.** Both follow the shape of the land as they flow or pass through it.

**Q32.** How is a spider web similar to a fishing net?  
**Ans.** Both use a network of lines to catch things.

**Q33.** How are leaf veins similar to a road network?  
**Ans.** Both branch out from a main line to reach many points.

**Q34.** Mention two natural designs and two manmade designs.  
**Ans.**
- Natural designs: spider web, seashell
- Manmade designs: bridge, market stall

### Exercise 8 — Scenario and discussion
1. Your school wants a new logo. How does design help?  
2. Draw or describe a simple pattern using only dots.  
3. Compare a seashell and a spiral staircase.  
4. **[Review]** What is biomimicry?  
5. **[Review]** What is design?

**Answers**
1. Design makes the logo clear, memorable, and meaningful.  
2. A pattern using only dots might be rows of dots of the same size repeated evenly.  
3. Both have a spiral form that grows outward from a centre.  
4. Biomimicry is copying ideas from nature to solve human problems.  
5. Design is planning how something looks and works.
